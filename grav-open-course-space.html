Grav Open Course Space

Full CMS

Grav Open Course Space is a free, open-source home on the web for one course or many, inside or outside your LMS – a ready-to-run Grav CMS skeleton package, with Markdown file-based content and a browser-based Admin panel, and no database. It’s the twin of Grav Open Publishing Space, and the free successor to Grav Open Course Hub and Open MultiCourse Hub, which will soon be in maintenance mode.

Open Course Space is a preview release (version 0.9): ready to try, with some things still being refined before 1.0. Want Helios’s app-like design later? Courses use the same page types and settings as Grav Helios Course Hub. Grav CMS Projects at a Glance shows every Grav project, free and Premium Helios, side by side.

Who It’s For

  • Educators building an open home for one course, or for several on one site
  • Instructors who want course content they can reuse from term to term, outside the LMS
  • Anyone who wants a free, fully open-source course site with no licence to buy
  • Individuals or teams that want a version-controlled, GitHub or Codeberg workflow

Built for Teaching and Learning

One course or many, each with its own space – giving learners what they need each week, and educators full control.

  • One course or many – a Courses page with a card for each course; with only one course, the logo goes straight to it
  • Each course its own space – a course navbar with the course’s name and pages, plus its own colour, header image, sidebar and footer
  • A clear weekly focus – a Home page with “What’s Happening This Week” reminders, plus schedule, resources and syllabus pages
  • Modules or weekly units – a Modules page with a card for each module, a list of modules beside every module page, and Previous and Next buttons; or weekly units, newest first
  • A blog, shared or per course – a site blog shared by all courses, or a course’s own
  • Learning content built in – Learning Objectives, Key Takeaways, reflections, callouts and badges, plus Google Slides, H5P, PDF and link card embeds
  • Course search – a search box beside each course’s pages searches just that course
  • Light, Dark & Auto modes – on Quark 2, Grav 2’s modern theme, with English, French and German
  • Accessible and private – keyboard navigation, labelled page regions for screen readers, and no third-party tracking built in

What Sets It Apart

  • LMS embedding without LTI – embed any page in Canvas, Moodle or Brightspace with a URL parameter; no LTI setup or plugin needed
  • Share content between courses – reuse any page or section across courses with the included Page Inject plugin
  • Learners keep their place – Keep My Place offers a Continue Reading link on the Modules page, back to the last module page they read
  • Reuse built in – an optional “This page as Markdown” link on pages, for anyone reusing your content
  • Moves to Premium Helios – the same page types and settings as Grav Helios Course Hub, so courses can move between the two
  • Ready for the 5Rs, no extra step – plain Markdown means your content is already ready to retain, reuse, revise, remix, and redistribute
  • No build pipeline – edit in the browser-based Admin panel or via Git; changes go live immediately

Moving Courses In

  • From your LMS – the companion Common Cartridge Converter (beta) turns a Canvas, Moodle, Brightspace or Blackboard export (.imscc) into an Open Course Space course, for you to review and refine (see an example)
  • From Docsify – the Grav Docsify Bridge (beta) moves courses between Docsify and Open Course Space, in either direction
  • From Open Course Hub or Open MultiCourse Hub – MultiCourse Hub courses move in unchanged, and an Open Course Hub course moves into a course folder of its own (see the README)

Write Your Courses in Markdown, Keep Your LMS

Open Course Space is not a learning management system. It holds your course content as Markdown files that you own and version with Git, while your LMS keeps handling enrollment, grading, and assessment.

  • Markdown content, outside the LMS – write and revise in plain Markdown files, then reuse them from term to term, or across Canvas, Moodle, and Brightspace
  • Embed any page in your LMS – a URL parameter displays only the page content

Behind the Scenes

  • Markdown files, not a database – content runs on simple text files, easier to back up, version control, and migrate than database-driven platforms
  • Open collaborative authoring – Git Sync keeps your files and live site matched, with “Edit this Page” links back to the Markdown source
  • Free and open source – MIT licensed, on the free Quark 2 Open Publishing theme, shared with Open Publishing Space; runs on Grav 2 (PHP 8.3+)

Why Grav CMS?

A mature, stable platform that just completed its biggest release yet – Grav 2 brings the completely new Admin 2 panel, a built-in API, AI-ready content and workflows, and support for the latest PHP versions. File-based content you fully control, no database required, and updates go live immediately with no build process required. Ten years on, 15.7k+ GitHub stars, and built for what’s next.

On Grav 2, even this free project gets the completely new Admin 2 panel, with a Getting Started guide in its sidebar.

What’s Included

  • Three example courses: CPT363-1 by modules, CPT363-2 with its own blog, and CPT363-3 by weekly units (the last two switched off, ready to turn on)
  • A Sample Course, switched off, as a starting point for your own
  • A site blog, an About the Courses page, and Getting Started in the Admin panel
  • A Read Me page describing every setting

Ready for Helios’s App-Like Design?

Grav Helios Course Hub is the Grav Premium Helios step up: one or several courses in a single install, with modules at the centre, sidebar navigation and a table of contents on pages. It needs the Grav Premium Helios theme, a paid theme sold by the Grav team at getgrav.org ($50 per site, one-time license) – its sales help fund Grav CMS development. Your course content moves across with a few adjustments.
